# KAY POS 0.2.32

- Enter serial numbers and warranty months from the cart before checkout; select a customer to link the warranties.
- Save the sale, stock, credit and serial registrations atomically. Prevent duplicate serials and quantity mismatches.
- Show serial numbers, warranty months and start/end dates on receipts, historic reprints and receipt details.
- Preserve serials through held carts and durable checkout recovery; retain compatibility with ordinary sales and older recovery data.
- Add A4 Warranty Form PDF export and A4 printing for registered serials, with shop logo/name, compact customer details, product/serial, warranty dates and signature/stamp spaces.
- Align shop branding and keep long content flowing across A4 pages. Refunded receipts show a warning.

Use the inventory icon beside the cart quantity controls to enter serials. In Sales > Serial & Warranty, select a registered unit and choose Export PDF or Print A4. Each warranty form covers one serialized unit. PDF export requires Windows Microsoft Print to PDF; printing opens the A4 printer chooser.
