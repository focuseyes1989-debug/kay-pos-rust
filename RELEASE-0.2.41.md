# KAY POS 0.2.41

- Group Sale Summary Location sales under their parent location, including parent stock and all nested child locations.
- Expand or collapse a parent to view the individual selling locations with quantities, allocated discount, net sales and cost.
- Count each receipt once within a parent even when it uses several child locations; preserve distinct receipt counts in the overall total.
- Keep older or unassigned location sales visible in the report.

Validated with nested parent rollup tests, multi-location receipt and discount allocation tests, standalone/core tests and desktop compilation.
